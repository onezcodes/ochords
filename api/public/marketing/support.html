Support

Quick answers for scanning, accounts, and Pro. Still stuck? Send feedback from the app or email us.

Scan didn’t read Myanmar lyrics

Sign in and try again when server reading is available. Guests and offline scans fall back to on-device OCR, which often can’t see Burmese — those lines become editable lyric placeholders with chords kept in place.

I lost access to my account

Use Forgot password in the app (email + password accounts). Sign in with Apple/Google uses the same provider account you linked. Social-only accounts may need a password set by support before using the staff panel — players should use the provider sign-in buttons.

Pro isn’t showing

Open the app while online so it can sync the latest StoreKit or Play purchase. Refunds and expiries update when the store notifies us or when the app syncs again.

Report a sheet or send an idea

Use Send feedback or Report inside O Chords. That reaches the moderation queue faster than email.

Contact

Email it@visibleone.com for account or privacy issues. Include your handle (not your password).
