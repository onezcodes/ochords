Privacy

O Chords is built to keep your music usable without treating personal data as product fuel. This page summarizes what the apps and API store today.

Account

If you create an account, we store your name, handle, and an encrypted email (looked up with a separate HMAC key). Passwords are hashed. Optional city and social sign-in subject IDs may be stored for profile and Apple/Google sign-in.

Your sheets

Songs you save sync to your account as chord-sheet data (title, artist, sections, visibility). Private notes on a sheet are encrypted and only returned to you. Public sheets appear in Explore.

Scanning

Photos used for sheet reading are processed in memory for the request. When server reading is on and you’re signed in, the image may be sent to Google Cloud Vision to read Myanmar lyrics. Scan photos are not kept on O Chords servers.

Reports & feedback

Free-text report details and feedback messages are encrypted at rest, retained for a limited period after resolution, and visible only to staff tools used for moderation.

Pro subscription

We store Pro expiry and the store’s original transaction identifier after verifying a purchase. We do not keep the full receipt payload.

Deletion & export

  • In-app account deletion starts a grace period, then removes the account and cascaded data.
  • Signed-in players can export their account data from the app.
